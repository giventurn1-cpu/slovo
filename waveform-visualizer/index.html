Waveform versus spectrum

A waveform draws the signal over time: one line, loud where the track is loud. A spectrum splits the sound into bands and shows each as a bar, so bass and treble move separately. Waves suit calm, melodic material and vertical formats; bars suit electronic and hip-hop, where the low end and the hats are doing different things and it shows.

What makes these different from a stock audiogram

Every one of these is drawn from the real analysis of your file, not a decorative animation. The ECG line spikes on actual transients. LIQUID bars spill drops when a hit exceeds the running level. TIMELINE draws a bar per moment so the last seconds of the song are visible as a shape. They can be layered over your photo, tinted to your accent colour, and combined with any other widget on this site.
