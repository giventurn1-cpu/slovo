Why the logo in the centre still works

It is the format streaming platforms trained everyone on: a square of art, a name, a title, and the sense that this is the official upload rather than a fan rip. The problem with the plain version is that nothing moves, and feeds drop it. These scenes keep the recognisable layout and give the art a pulse, so it reads as official and as alive at the same time.

Eight ways to make art move

LOGO PULSE rings the art with spectrum. OFFICIAL is the clean card. GOLD builds the logo from thousands of particles and blows it apart on the drop. BOLT hits it with lightning on the kick. HORIZON runs two oscilloscope lines through it. PLAYER puts it on a CRT with VU meters and glitch. ORBITS and MOSAIC turn it into a 3D object. Same asset, eight moods.

Add your own art
