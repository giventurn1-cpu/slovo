SLOVO — сторонние модели и библиотеки / third-party models and libraries
=======================================================================

Всё считается в браузере. Модели качаются, только когда нужны, и только веса —
ваши файлы никуда не уходят.
Everything runs in your browser. Models are downloaded only when needed
(weights only); your files never leave the device.

Модели / Models
---------------

Depth Anything V2 Small — карта глубины для «настоящего 3D» / depth map for "real 3D"
  Authors: Lihe Yang, Bingyi Kang, Zilong Huang, Zhen Zhao, Xiaogang Xu,
  Jiashi Feng, Hengshuang Zhao.
  License: Apache License 2.0.
  Weights (ONNX, onnx-community): https://huggingface.co/onnx-community/depth-anything-v2-small
  Original: https://huggingface.co/depth-anything/Depth-Anything-V2-Small

MODNet — автовырезание объекта / object cut-out
  Authors: Zhanghan Ke, Jiayu Sun, Kaican Li, Qiong Yan, Rynson W.H. Lau
  (AAAI 2022).
  License: Apache License 2.0 (code and models).
  Code: https://github.com/ZHKKKe/MODNet
  Weights (ONNX, Xenova): https://huggingface.co/Xenova/modnet

Beat This! (small0) — доли и такты для сборки из фото / beats and bars for photo sequences
  Authors: Francesco Foscarin, Jan Schlüter, Gerhard Widmer — Institute of
  Computational Perception, JKU Linz (ISMIR 2024).
  License: MIT (code and weights), full text: models/LICENSE-beat-this.txt
  Code and weights: https://github.com/CPJKU/beat_this
  ONNX: converted by SLOVO from the official small0 checkpoint; served from this site.

MI-GAN (512, Places2) — дорисовка фона за объектом для «настоящего 3D» / background fill behind the subject for "real 3D"
  Authors: Andranik Sargsyan, Shant Navasardyan, Xingqian Xu, Humphrey Shi —
  Picsart AI Research (ICCV 2023).
  License: MIT (code and weights), full text: models/LICENSE-migan.txt
  Code: https://github.com/Picsart-AI-Research/MI-GAN
  Weights (ONNX pipeline by the authors): https://huggingface.co/andraniksargsyan/migan
