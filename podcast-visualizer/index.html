The audiogram, done properly

RIBBON, STROKES and COMB are the classic audiogram shapes — a filled wave, vertical strokes, a dense comb — drawn from the real analysis of the voice rather than a looping animation. Put them along the bottom of a photo of the guest or the studio, leave the upper two-thirds for the title and a pull quote, and you have the format every podcast clip uses, without a subscription.

The studio look

RECORDER shows studio timecode and left/right level meters; NEEDLE is an analogue VU with inertia and overshoot; SCALE is a level strip in decibels; REC adds a recording indicator. Over a dark photo they read as a control room, which is the honest visual for a talk show. CREDITS runs a line of text with a hairline of progress under it — episode number, guest, date.

Words on screen

A large share of feed viewing is muted, so the clip must read without sound. Paste the key lines of the episode and SLOVO times them to the voice — the same lyric engine, applied to speech. CALLOUT points a caption at a spot in the frame; COMMENTS and TICKER add a live-show feel. Full episodes export at any length; for a feed clip, trim to the best forty seconds.

FAQ

Does SLOVO transcribe the episode? No. There is no speech recognition — you paste the lines you want on screen and SLOVO times them to the voice. For a full transcript, use a transcription tool first. Can I export a full one-hour episode? Long exports work, but the comfortable range is up to about twelve minutes per file; for a full episode export in parts and join them, or make a static-plus-audiogram version. Which shape for a clip with text? RIBBON or COMB along the bottom edge, inside the platform safe zone, with the title and quote above.
