What is a Spotify Canvas?

A Canvas is the short looping video that plays behind your song in the Spotify app instead of the static cover. Artists use it because it makes a track feel alive — and Spotify has said Canvas can lift streams and saves. To upload one in Spotify for Artists it has to match a strict spec:

Aspect ratio9:16 (vertical)
Length3–8 seconds, loops seamlessly
Resolution720×1280 or higher
FileMP4, no sound

SLOVO's Canvas mode exports exactly this — you don't have to worry about the numbers.

What makes a Canvas loop work

A Canvas is three to eight seconds long and repeats forever while somebody listens, which makes it a very different object from a music video. Two rules follow from that. It has to loop without a visible seam, because the join is the thing a repeated clip trains the eye to find. And it has to survive being watched a hundred times, which means slow, hypnotic motion beats a fast cut every time — anything frantic becomes irritating by the third play.

Silent by design

The loop carries no audio: the listener already has the song playing. That is why the export here is built as a silent file rather than a video with the sound stripped out afterwards. It also means the visual has to carry the mood alone, which is a good argument for using your own artwork or a photograph rather than a generic template.

Use your own frame — in real 3D

Cover art works, but a still photograph in 3D often works better. No track needed: a video without music

A Canvas has no sound, so you do not even need to load the song. Choose a video without music, type the length — 8 seconds is the classic choice — and export: the file comes out with no audio track at all, which is exactly what Spotify asks for. Turn the beat switch off in the top bar and nothing pulses; the photo just drifts in depth, which is what a loop that plays behind a song for minutes should do.

Three looks that work as a Canvas

Depth. 3D PHOTO on the cover photo or a portrait — the slowest and most “filmed” option, and the one that survives hours of looping without getting tiring. Atmosphere. SMOKE or LIVE PHOTO on a portrait: drifting smoke with light behind the head, or a frame whose bright parts breathe softly. The cover itself. OFFICIAL keeps the square art in the centre, the way listeners recognise a release, over a background that slowly sinks into the depth. Avoid anything that flashes or cuts: on loop it becomes tiring, and it runs into Spotify’s content guidelines.
