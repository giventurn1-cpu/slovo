Why most free status apps disappoint

The typical lyrical status app on the Play Store is free to install and then asks you to pay at the last step: a logo stamped in the corner, a “remove watermark” button that plays an ad or opens a subscription, or a quality lock until you upgrade. It also takes storage on your phone and often wants permissions it does not need.

SLOVO works differently. It is a website that builds the video inside your own browser, so it pays no server bill per video and has nothing to charge you for. No watermark, no ads in the video, no limit, no account. And because nothing is rendered on a server, your song is never uploaded anywhere.

Lyrics timed in one listen

The part that takes longest in any lyrical video is the timing — sliding every line to the right second. Here it takes one listen: paste the words, play the song and tap as each line begins — the tap button on a phone, the space bar on a computer. Missed one? Step back a line. Afterwards every line can be dragged or stretched on the subtitle track. Show one line at a time, or let the Karaoke style fill each line from left to right while it is on screen. It works for Hindi, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, English or any other language, because the words and the timing both come from you.

One honest note: SLOVO does not transcribe songs and does not guess the timing. Over music, speech recognition gets lyrics wrong, and it would need your audio on someone else's server. You paste the words, you tap the timing — and the song stays on your phone. Already have an LRC or SRT file with timings? Paste it and they apply as they are.

Your photo, moving with the music

Use a selfie, a couple photo, a picture of the artist, or a short video clip as the background. The scene reacts to the song: a slow zoom for romantic tracks, a pulse on every beat for Punjabi and hip-hop, glitch and VHS for something harder. 3D Photo builds real depth from the picture right in your browser: it separates you from the background, fills in what was hidden behind you and moves the camera around you — the look of a filmed shot from a single photo. Slowed + reverb, lo-fi and Bollywood edits

Sized for WhatsApp Status, Reels and Shorts

Export in vertical 9:16, which fits WhatsApp Status, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. WhatsApp's help centre currently gives 90 seconds as the status limit, and longer videos have to be split — but a status is watched in a tap, so the hook alone, 20 to 40 seconds, usually lands better: trim straight to it before exporting. The editor marks the areas that Reels and Shorts cover with their buttons, so your lyrics stay visible. The same project also exports 16:9 for a full YouTube lyrical video.

On a budget phone

SLOVO runs in Chrome on Android. On faster phones export is quick; on budget phones and on iPhone it runs at roughly the length of the clip — a 30-second status takes about 30 seconds, a full song takes as long as the song. Choose 720p, which is lighter and looks good on Status, and keep the screen on without switching apps until it finishes.

Light on data

The first visit downloads about 1–1.5 MB, and after that the site opens from cache almost without using data. Only two features download larger files — 3D Photo (about 50 MB, once) and cutting an object out of a photo — and only when you use them.
