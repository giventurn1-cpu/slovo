The structural difference

Everything else follows from one fact. Specterr renders your video on its servers. That is a real cost per video, and it has to be recovered — which is why the free tier carries a watermark, a resolution cap and a quota, and why the paid tiers exist. None of that is unreasonable; it is what server rendering costs.

SLOVO has no rendering backend. The audio analysis, the visuals and the H.264 encoder all run in your browser, on your own hardware. There is no per-video bill, so there is nothing to recover with a watermark or a subscription — and nowhere to upload your track to. For an unreleased master, that last point is often the one that decides.

Where Specterr wins

Two places, and both are real. First, server rendering does not care how old your laptop is: a 4K export takes the same time on a ten-year-old machine as on a new one. SLOVO renders on your device — fast on a modern desktop, slower on an old one, and phones fall back to a slower path. Second, Specterr has a much larger template catalogue, including 3D scenes, and a pick-a-template-type-a-title workflow that is hard to beat for speed when you do not want to tune anything.

Where SLOVO wins

No watermark at any resolution up to 4K, no quota, no account, and the track never leaves your device. Beyond the free tier, the visuals are driven by separate bass, transient and spectrum analysis rather than overall loudness, so a scene jolts on the kick and breathes with the bassline instead of twitching on everything. Lyrics are timed to the vocal after you paste them, without speech recognition and therefore without uploading audio. And the same project exports 16:9, 9:16 and 1:1, a Spotify Canvas loop and a YouTube thumbnail.

Which to pick

Pick Specterr if your machine is slow, you want hundreds of ready templates, or you are happy to pay for server rendering and 3D scenes.

Pick SLOVO if you need watermark-free exports without paying, you care that the track stays on your device, or you want lyrics and multiple aspect ratios from one project.

Try both — SLOVO takes a minute with no sign-up, so the cheapest way to decide is to run the same track through each and look.
