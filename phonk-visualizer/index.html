What a phonk visual needs

Weight on the low end and violence on the hit. The engine reads the sub-bass separately from the transients, so the camera shake rides the 808 while the glitch tears land on the cowbell or the kick, not on both at once. Most generic visualizers use overall loudness for everything and end up jittering constantly — the opposite of the slow-heavy pump the genre lives on.

Drift, smoke, night

PHONK DRIFT is the ring, the smoke and the shake. TRAP NIGHT is the triangle, the gold and the rain. ECLIPSE and SMOKE are the cinematic darkness. GLITCH, VHS and STROBE take your own photo — a car, a portrait, a city at night — and give it the 90s-tape or datamosh treatment on the beat. FRACTURE tears a clean frame with a glowing crack.
