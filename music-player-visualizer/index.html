Why a player interface makes a good video

It tells the viewer what they are looking at without a caption: this is a song, it is playing, here is how far in we are. Progress and timecode are information, not decoration, and people watch longer when a video is legible. The retro variants add nostalgia on top — the cassette and the pocket player are instantly placed in time.

Instruments: the studio look

NEEDLE is an analogue VU meter with inertia and overshoot — it lags and swings the way a real one does, which is what makes it look real. PEAK METER holds a peak mark that slowly slides. SCALE is a level strip in decibels. RADAR sweeps in a circle and leaves fading marks on the hits. Over a dark photo these read as a control room.

Cover art in the player

RING PLAYER and CASSETTE take your cover and put it where a real player would: in the circle, on the label. Title and artist go into the display. The result looks like a screenshot of something that exists.

FAQ
