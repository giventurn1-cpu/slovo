Weight on the 808

The engine reads the sub-bass separately from the transients, so the picture can pump with the 808 while the hits land on the snare — instead of everything twitching on overall loudness. ECLIPSE and SMOKE breathe with the low end; GLITCH tears on the hit; TO THE DROP counts down to the next one, which keeps the feed watching.

Type as the visual

NUMBER puts a huge track number in the corner and TAG adds a barcode and a tech line — duration, format, date. Over a dark photo they read as a mixtape cover, and they carry the video when the art is not the point. HEARTS throws a volley of reactions on the drop for the feed version.
