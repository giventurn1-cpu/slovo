When less is the right call

Ambient, lo-fi, piano, a ballad, a podcast — anything where a lightning storm would be embarrassing. The minimal widgets are built to sit on top of your own picture or a plain dark frame and add exactly one thing: proof that the video is alive and in time with the sound. They also stack — several widgets can be layered into one scene, each reacting on its own.

Reads well on a phone

Thin lines, high contrast, nothing busy. Minimal scenes survive being watched at the size of a hand, which is where most feed views happen, and they leave room for text — a lyric line or a title never competes with the visual.

PURE and CONSTRUCTOR

PURE is the empty frame: your photo or video, no effect at all, for when the picture is the point and only the export matters. CONSTRUCTOR is the other extreme — assemble your own scene from layers, shapes, text and modulators when nothing ready-made fits.

FAQ

Can I combine several minimal widgets? Yes. Widgets stack as layers in one scene, each with its own reaction and opacity. Does it work over my own video? Yes. Minimal widgets are designed to sit over a photo or a video background. Is there a completely clean option? PURE — no effect, just your frame, exported in any aspect ratio.
