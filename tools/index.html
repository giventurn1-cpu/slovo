Why it can be free without a watermark

Most online video tools rent servers to render your file, so the free tier has to cost you something — a watermark, a queue, an account, a resolution cap. SLOVO has no rendering backend at all: the analysis, the visuals and the MP4 encoder run in your own browser. There is no server bill to recover, so there is nothing to upsell you. That is also why your track never gets uploaded — there is nowhere to upload it to.
