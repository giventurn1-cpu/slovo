Not a generator

These effects never invent pixels. A generative model would turn the head or blink the eyes — and quietly turn the face into someone else. Here the photograph is a fixed plate; the engine moves it, layers it, cuts it, lights it. The result stays recognisably yours from the first frame to the last, which for a press shot or an album cover is the whole point.

Depth is the one that changes everything

DEPTH separates the subject from the background in your browser and moves them at different speeds. The background drifts, the subject holds, and a flat picture gains volume the way a real camera move does. Pair it with a slow reaction setting and a portrait becomes a cinematic opening shot; pair it with GLITCH or STROBE ECHO and it becomes a phonk visual.

Picking the right photo

Sharp, high resolution, one clear subject, room around the edges. The camera pushes in, so anything touching the frame edge leaves it. A portrait with a clean edge against the background separates best. A small re-compressed screenshot is the worst input — motion only advertises the softness.

FAQ

Does it change my face or body? No. Nothing is redrawn — the photo is moved, layered and lit. It stays recognisable throughout. Is the photo uploaded anywhere? No. Depth separation and every effect run in your browser; the image never leaves your device. Which effect for a calm song? DEPTH or LIVING PHOTO with the reaction turned down. For hard tracks: GLITCH, STROBE ECHO, VHS.
