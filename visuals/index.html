How the previews were made

Every clip on these pages was rendered by the same engine that exports your video, from one synthetic four-bar beat at 142 BPM, in the same 1:1 frame, and then downscaled. Nothing was hand-animated or cherry-picked, so a scene that looks calm here will look calm on your track, and one that hits here will hit. Open any scene in the editor and drop your own file to see it on your music.
